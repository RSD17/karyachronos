Get to Know Kacy

Mission Statement

Kacy aims to reduce academic stress by promoting and actively implementing healthy scheduling habits for effective workload management, helping maintain balance in a student's life and reduce feelings of overwhelmedness.

About Kacy

Kacy is an algorithm-based platform that allows students ranging from middle-school to college effectively manage their workload by neatly organizing tasks, generated routine plans, and generated exam preperation schedules. Designed with students at the core, Kacy aims to help students balance academic success and mental well-being.

What makes Kacy Unique

Kacy diverges from the idea of constantly maintaining and manually updating static schedules, but rather implements algorithmic logic to actively categorize tasks based on priority to dymanically update a flexible schedule. Moreover, having being entirely developed by a high-school student, Kacy has been trained using data personalized to be highly student-centered to optimize workload management.
